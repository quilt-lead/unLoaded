## What it is

The model panel (formerly "3D Structural Model") has two tabs, **3D** and **Plan**. The plan shows one floor level as a
framing plan with level stepping: the ▲/▼ buttons, the level ladder, Page Up / Page Down, or `[` / `]`. Both tabs
stay mounted, and the inactive one is `hidden`. Unmounting the 3D view would drop the WebGL context and reload the
IFC.

## Decisions and findings

- **SVG from element data, not mesh cuts (the user chose this).** The plan uses `bbox`, plus `axis` and `section`
  (now passed through by `elementGraphToBim`), so it also works for IFC-less model bundles. An export JSON has no
  geometry, so it shows an empty state.
- **Levels are geometric, not by storey name.** In IFC, and in the Harborview data, a storey's beams and slab are
  the *ceiling* of that storey. The "G-D4E4-L03" girder and "Slab L03" sit at the L04 elevation. Levels are the
  storeys (`ElementTopology.levels`) plus any slab no storey sits on, which is how the Harborview roof gets the level
  "Slab L06". Levels with nothing to draw are dropped.
- **The rules per element kind are in the header of `planSlice.ts`.** All the tolerances are in `PLAN_TOLERANCES`.
  They were tuned on Harborview: 20 cut columns per floor, every beam, column and connection lands on a level, and
  no element appears twice on one level.
- **Column conventions:** a column is cut at E − 0.5 m. These are the columns *below* the floor, so the marks are the
  storey-below ones, e.g. C-01-L01 on L02. A column that starts at E with nothing below it is drawn dashed. This
  covers transfer columns and the ground floor.
- **Selection behaviour:**
  - A selection made elsewhere moves the plan to a level that element is drawn on (`levelForElement`; it keeps the
    current level if the element is there).
  - The plan only pans when the selection is off screen. FIT zooms to it.
  - If the selection is drawn only on other levels, a "Selection is on L0x" chip offers to jump there.
- **Auto-framing:** the plan stays framed to the whole model through resizes and docking until the user pans or
  zooms. Framing leaves room for the level control (except in compact panels narrower than 480 px) and for the
  status strip.
- **Shared emphasis rules:** the dim and lit rules of the 3D view moved to `utils/elementEmphasis.ts`, so the plan
  and 3D can't drift apart. The 3D output is unchanged.
